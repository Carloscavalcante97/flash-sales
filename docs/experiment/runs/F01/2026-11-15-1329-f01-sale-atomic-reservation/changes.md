## What changed
- `apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma`: appended the `Sale` and `Order` models and the `OrderStatus` enum, verbatim from the spec.
- `apps/api/prisma/migrations/20261115155150_f01_sales_orders/migration.sql`: generated by `prisma migrate dev --name f01_sales_orders` and applied to the local docker Postgres. Not hand-edited. It contains `"status" "OrderStatus" NOT NULL DEFAULT 'RESERVED'`.
- `apps/api/prisma/migrations/migration_lock.toml`: generated by Prisma (`provider = "postgresql"`).
- `apps/api/src/sales.service.ts`: new `SalesService`. `create` is `prisma.sale.create`. `findOne` is `findUnique` and throws `NotFoundException` on null. `reserve` is the single-statement CTE `$executeRaw` copied exactly from the spec. It returns `{ orderId, status: 'RESERVED' }` on 1 row inserted. Otherwise it calls `findOne`, which gives 404 for an unknown sale, and then throws `ConflictException({ error: 'SOLD_OUT' })`.
- `apps/api/src/sales.controller.ts`: new `SalesController` with `POST /sales`, `GET /sales/:id` and `POST /sales/:id/orders`. Validation is inline and runs before any service call. Bodies are read through `body?.x`. The checks follow the spec: `name` and `buyerId` must be non-blank strings, and `stock` must be an integer from 1 to 2147483647. On failure it throws `BadRequestException`. Only `name`, `stock` and `buyerId` are read.
- `apps/api/src/app.module.ts`: registered `SalesController` and `SalesService` next to `AppController` and `AppService`.

- A sale created with `"id":"forced","extra":1` came back with a real uuid and no `extra` key.
- With stock 1, the SQL-injection `buyerId` got 201, the next order got `409 {"error":"SOLD_OUT"}`, and GET returned `stock: 0`.
- `/sales/abc` returned 404. `POST /sales/abc/orders` returned 404 with a valid body and 400 with `{}`.
- POST with no body, with `[]`, and with `stock` 2147483648 each returned 400.
- 200 concurrent curl orders against stock 50 gave exactly 50 × 201 and 150 × 409, with final stock 0.

These checks are not tests. They left a few rows in the local dev DB.
